Markie base character asset
===========================

Source: Animated Human by Quaternius
https://opengameart.org/content/animated-human-low-poly

License: CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication)
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The model is used as the rigged COLLADA (.dae) base for Markie in Scraper Life.
Its material is styled in-game for the prototype. Future production should
replace or further customise it with an original, Liberian-reviewed Markie model.
